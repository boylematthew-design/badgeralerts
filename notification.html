Page speed is below 70

Your LCP is currently at 3.20s. This should be improved.

Why this is important

A slow website can quietly damage performance across the board. When pages take too long to load, visitors are more likely to bounce before they even see your offer, which means fewer leads, fewer enquiries and fewer sales. It can also make your brand feel less trustworthy and less polished, especially on mobile where people expect near-instant loading. On top of that, weak page speed can hurt your visibility in search, because performance signals are part of the overall experience Google looks at.

How to fix this

Start by looking at the biggest elements loading above the fold, because LCP is often dragged down by oversized images, slow server response times or heavy scripts. Compress large images, use next-gen formats where possible, remove any unnecessary third-party tools, and make sure important content loads before less critical page elements. You should also check whether unused CSS or JavaScript is slowing the page down. In many cases, tightening up the hero section, improving hosting response times and reducing render-blocking assets can bring the score back into a much healthier range.
